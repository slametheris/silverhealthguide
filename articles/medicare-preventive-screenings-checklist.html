Frequently Asked Questions

What preventive screenings are free with Medicare?

Medicare covers at $0: cardiovascular screenings, colorectal cancer (colonoscopy every 10 years, FIT annually), mammogram annually (women), lung cancer CT for eligible smokers, Pap test every 2 years, bone density DEXA every 2 years, diabetes screening, depression screening, HIV, hepatitis B and C, alcohol misuse counseling, and all recommended vaccines.

How often does Medicare pay for a mammogram?

Every 12 months for women over 40 — $0 copay or deductible when done for screening.

Does Medicare cover bone density tests?

Every 24 months for postmenopausal women at risk for osteoporosis, long-term steroid users, and those with vertebral abnormalities.

Medicare covers over 20 preventive screenings at $0 — but Medicare doesn't send reminders. You must ask. This checklist tells you exactly what to request.

✅ Every Year

  • Annual Wellness Visit — request by name, $0
  • Flu vaccine — $0 under Part D every fall
  • COVID-19 vaccine — $0 per updated schedule
  • Mammogram (women) — annually after age 40
  • Depression screening — included in Annual Wellness Visit
  • Diabetes screening — up to twice/year if at risk
  • FIT colon test — annually as colonoscopy alternative
  • Lung cancer CT — annually if 50–77, heavy smoker/former smoker

✅ Every 2 Years

  • Bone density scan (DEXA) — postmenopausal women at risk
  • Pap smear (women) — every 24 months
  • Hepatitis C screening — every 12 months if at risk

✅ Every 10 Years

  • Colonoscopy — average risk. Every 2 years if high-risk.

✅ Once / As Needed

  • Shingrix (shingles) — 2 doses, $0 under Part D
  • Pneumococcal vaccine — discuss schedule with doctor
  • Hepatitis B vaccine — if not previously vaccinated
  • RSV vaccine (Abrysvo) — adults 60+
  • AAA ultrasound — once for men 65–75 who ever smoked

How to Get All Your Screenings

At your Annual Wellness Visit, bring this checklist and ask: "Which screenings am I due for?" Your doctor documents your prevention plan and orders needed screenings. Keep a record of completion dates. All vaccines are $0 under Part D — ask your pharmacist directly without a doctor visit.

  • Schedule Annual Wellness Visit — use those exact words
  • Bring this checklist to your appointment
  • Ask: "Which screenings am I due for this year?"
  • Record the date of each completed screening
