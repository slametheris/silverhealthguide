Missed Medicare's December 7 Deadline? Your Options for 2027

MC
By Margaret Chen, PharmD
🕐 7 min read
✅ Expert reviewed

Missing December 7 doesn't always mean you're locked in for the year. Several windows can still let you change coverage, depending on your situation. Here is what's available — and what isn't.

1. Medicare Advantage Open Enrollment (January 1–March 31)

If you are already in a Medicare Advantage plan, you can make one change between January 1 and March 31: switch to a different Medicare Advantage plan, or leave Medicare Advantage for Original Medicare and join a standalone Part D plan. You cannot use this period to move from Original Medicare into Medicare Advantage.

2. Special Enrollment Periods (SEPs)

A qualifying life event can open a window to change plans, typically about two months. Common triggers include:

  • Moving outside your plan's service area, or to a place with new plan options
  • Losing employer or union coverage
  • Your plan leaving Medicare or your service area
  • Moving into or out of a nursing home or long-term care facility
  • Gaining or losing Medicaid or Extra Help eligibility — special rules apply and have changed in recent years, so confirm with 1-800-MEDICARE or your SHIP

3. The 5-Star Option

If a Medicare Advantage or Part D plan in your area holds a 5-star overall rating, you can generally switch into it once during the year outside of AEP. Check Plan Finder to see whether one is available where you live.

4. Medigap

Medigap isn't tied to AEP. You can apply any time, but outside your Medigap Open Enrollment Period insurers in most states can review your health history and may charge more or decline you. See Medigap enrollment timing.

5. If You Have No Drug Coverage

If you don't have creditable drug coverage and miss the window, you may owe a late-enrollment penalty when you do join, and you may have to wait for the next enrollment opportunity. Ask about Extra Help if your income and resources are limited — it can lower drug costs and affect your enrollment options.

What to Do Next

  1. Identify which situation fits you: already in Medicare Advantage, in Original Medicare, or experiencing a life event
  2. Call 1-800-MEDICARE or your SHIP to confirm which window applies to you
  3. Set a reminder for January 1 (Advantage Open Enrollment) and for next October 15
  4. Review your plan's costs in January and make a note of anything that surprises you
  • Already in Medicare Advantage? Use the January 1–March 31 window if the plan isn't working
  • Had a life event? Ask about a Special Enrollment Period within two months
  • In Original Medicare? You generally can't join Medicare Advantage until the next AEP unless an SEP applies
  • Don't pay anyone for enrollment help — SHIP counseling is free

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I switch Medicare plans after December 7?

Sometimes. If you are in a Medicare Advantage plan, you can make one change between January 1 and March 31. Special Enrollment Periods apply after certain life events. Otherwise, the next general window is October 15, 2027.

Can I move from Original Medicare to Medicare Advantage in January?

Generally no. The January–March period is only for people already in a Medicare Advantage plan. You would need a Special Enrollment Period or the next Annual Enrollment Period.

Is there a penalty for missing open enrollment?

There is no penalty for simply not changing plans. Penalties apply when you delay first enrolling in Part B or Part D without creditable coverage.
