Medicare Coverage for CGM: Dexcom G7, FreeStyle Libre, and Diabetes Supplies 2026

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Medicare cover CGM?

Yes — Medicare Part B covers CGMs including Dexcom G7 and FreeStyle Libre 3 for insulin-using beneficiaries as durable medical equipment. You pay 20% coinsurance after the Part B deductible.

Who qualifies for Medicare CGM coverage?

You must have diabetes, use insulin (injections or pump), and have your doctor write a prescription documenting medical necessity. Both Type 1 and Type 2 diabetics on insulin qualify.

Does Medicare cover FreeStyle Libre?

Yes — Medicare covers FreeStyle Libre 3 for insulin-using beneficiaries under Part B as durable medical equipment.

What is the insulin cost cap under Medicare?

Under Medicare Part D in 2026, insulin is capped at $35/month per covered insulin product regardless of tier.

Medicare Part B now covers continuous glucose monitors — including the Dexcom G7 and FreeStyle Libre 3 — as durable medical equipment for insulin users. Here is exactly how to access this benefit.

Who Qualifies

To qualify: you have diabetes, you use insulin (injections or pump), and your doctor writes a prescription documenting medical necessity. Type 1 and Type 2 diabetics on insulin both qualify. Type 2 diabetics managed without insulin do not currently qualify for CGM coverage.

All Covered Diabetes Supplies

  • CGM devices and sensors — insulin users
  • Blood glucose monitors — all diabetics
  • Test strips — 100/month non-insulin, 300/month insulin users
  • Lancets and lancet devices
  • Insulin pumps and pump insulin (Part B)
  • Therapeutic shoes — 1 pair/year with peripheral neuropathy

$35 Insulin Cap Under Part D

Starting in 2023 and continuing in 2026, insulin is capped at $35/month per product under Medicare Part D — regardless of which tier it's on. This has dramatically reduced insulin costs for seniors with diabetes.

How to Get Your CGM

  1. Ask your doctor: "Am I eligible for a CGM through Medicare?"
  2. Your doctor writes a prescription with medical necessity documentation
  3. Contact a Medicare-enrolled DME supplier
  4. Pay 20% coinsurance after Part B deductible ($0 with Medigap Plan G)
  • Ask your doctor about CGM eligibility at your next visit
  • Verify your insulin is on your Part D formulary — cap applies to covered insulins
  • Schedule annual diabetic retinopathy screening — free under Medicare
