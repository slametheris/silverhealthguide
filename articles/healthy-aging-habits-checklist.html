Healthy Aging Checklist: 20 Evidence-Based Habits for a Better Life After 60

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the most important habits for healthy aging?

Regular aerobic exercise (30 min most days), strength training 2x/week, 7–8 hours of sleep, treating hearing loss, controlling blood pressure, social engagement, not smoking, limiting alcohol, anti-inflammatory diet, and annual preventive healthcare visits.

Can you reverse aging effects with lifestyle changes?

Some effects are reversible: strength training reverses muscle loss, aerobic exercise grows the hippocampus, treating hearing loss slows cognitive decline, blood pressure control reduces stroke risk. Meaningful improvement is documented at any age.

What preventive checks should seniors have annually?

Annual wellness visit, blood pressure, cholesterol, blood glucose/A1c, mammogram (women), colorectal cancer screening, bone density every 2 years (postmenopausal women), eye exam, hearing test, dental cleaning, skin cancer check, and all recommended vaccines.

Longevity research is clear: how you age is largely within your control. These 20 habits represent lifestyle changes with the greatest impact on lifespan, cognition, and quality of life after 60.

✅ Movement and Fitness

  • Walk 30 minutes daily — the most accessible longevity intervention
  • Strength train 2 days per week — reverses muscle loss at any age
  • Balance exercises 3x per week — reduces fall risk by up to 40%
  • Break sitting every 30–45 minutes — move briefly between sitting periods
  • Replace walking shoes annually — worn shoes are a fall risk

✅ Nutrition

  • Fatty fish twice weekly — omega-3 protects heart and brain
  • Leafy greens 5+ times per week — highest-evidence food for brain health
  • 6–8 cups fluid daily — dehydration is the most underestimated risk
  • Eliminate sugary beverages — replace with water, tea, black coffee
  • Berries 3+ times per week — anti-inflammatory, brain-protective

✅ Sleep and Stress

  • Protect 7–8 hours of sleep — brain clears Alzheimer's proteins during deep sleep
  • Consistent wake time every day — the most powerful sleep intervention
  • Ask your doctor about sleep apnea — affects 20–30% of seniors undiagnosed
  • 10 minutes of deep breathing daily — reduces cortisol measurably

✅ Preventive Healthcare

  • Annual Wellness Visit every year — request it by name, it's free
  • Know your blood pressure — check at home if elevated
  • Get a hearing test — treating hearing loss is the #1 dementia prevention action
  • Annual medication review — polypharmacy is a major senior risk
  • All recommended vaccines — flu, COVID, Shingrix, pneumococcal

✅ Social and Mental

  • Maintain 2+ meaningful social connections — isolation is as harmful as smoking
  • Learn one genuinely new skill per year — builds cognitive reserve
