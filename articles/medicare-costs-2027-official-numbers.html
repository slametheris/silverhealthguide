Medicare Costs in 2027: Official Part B, Part A, Part D, and IRMAA Numbers

MC
By Margaret Chen, PharmD
🕐 8 min read
✅ Expert reviewed

On November 17, 2026, CMS released the official 2027 Medicare Part A and Part B premiums, deductibles, and coinsurance. The standard Part B premium rises to $209.40 a month. Here is every key number, next to what you pay in 2026, so you can budget for January.

Part B: Premium and Deductible

Item20262027
Standard monthly premium$202.90$209.40 (+$6.50)
Annual deductible$283$292 (+$9)

At $209.40 a month, Part B costs about $2,513 a year per person. After the deductible, Original Medicare generally pays 80% of covered Part B services and you pay 20% (or your Medigap plan does).

Part A: Hospital and Skilled Nursing Costs

Item20262027
Inpatient hospital deductible (per benefit period)$1,736$1,776
Hospital days 61–90 (per day)$434$444
Lifetime reserve days (per day)$868$888
Skilled nursing facility days 21–100 (per day)$217$222

About 99% of beneficiaries pay no Part A premium because they have at least 40 quarters of Medicare-covered work. Those who must buy Part A pay $328 a month with 30–39 quarters (up from $311) or $597 with fewer than 30 quarters (up from $565).

Part D: Cap, Deductible, and Base Premium

  • Out-of-pocket cap: $2,400 in 2027 (was $2,100 in 2026). After you spend this much on covered drugs you pay $0 for covered Part D drugs for the rest of the year.
  • Maximum deductible: $700 (was $615). Many plans charge less, and some have no deductible for certain drugs.
  • National base beneficiary premium: $41.33 (was $38.99). This is a benchmark used for late-enrollment penalties and income surcharges, not the premium you will pay. Your plan's premium varies.

What to Do With These Numbers

  • Budget about $2,513 for Part B in 2027 — more if IRMAA applies to you
  • If you have Medigap Plan G, plan for the $292 Part B deductible
  • Check your Part D plan's 2027 deductible (up to $700) and premium in your Annual Notice of Change
  • If your income fell, consider an IRMAA appeal
  • Open enrollment ends December 7 — compare plans before then

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Medicare Part B premium for 2027?

The standard Part B premium for 2027 is $209.40 per month, an increase of $6.50 from $202.90 in 2026. The annual Part B deductible is $292, up from $283.

What is the Medicare Part A deductible for 2027?

The 2027 Part A inpatient hospital deductible is $1,776 per benefit period, up $40 from $1,736 in 2026. Days 61–90 cost $444 per day and lifetime reserve days cost $888 per day.

What is the 2027 Part D out-of-pocket cap?

The 2027 Part D annual out-of-pocket cap is $2,400, up from $2,100 in 2026. The maximum plan deductible rises to $700. Premiums do not count toward the cap, and neither does spending on drugs your plan does not cover.

When do the 2027 costs take effect?

All of these amounts apply to calendar year 2027, beginning January 1, 2027. Your 2026 amounts continue through December 31, 2026.
