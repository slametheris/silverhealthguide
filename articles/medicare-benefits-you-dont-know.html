10 Medicare Benefits Most Seniors Never Use (But Should)

Frequently Asked Questions

What free benefits does Medicare offer?

Medicare covers free preventive benefits including Annual Wellness Visit, cardiovascular screenings, diabetes screenings, colonoscopy, mammogram, lung CT, depression screening, bone density, HIV and hepatitis screenings, alcohol misuse counseling, and all recommended vaccines.

What is the Medicare Annual Wellness Visit?

A free yearly prevention-focused appointment — health risk assessment, medication review, blood pressure, depression and cognitive screening, and a personalized prevention plan. Entirely free under Part B, no copay or deductible.

Does Medicare cover gym memberships?

Original Medicare does not. However, many Medicare Advantage plans include fitness benefits — SilverSneakers, Silver&Fit, or One Pass. Call your plan and ask: 'What fitness benefits am I entitled to?'

The average Medicare beneficiary uses fewer than half the benefits they've paid for. These 10 underused benefits are free or nearly free — most seniors simply don't know they exist.

10 Underused Benefits

1. Annual Wellness Visit — $0 Every Year

This prevention-focused visit is entirely free. Call your doctor and say exactly: "I want to schedule my Medicare Annual Wellness Visit." Many doctors don't offer it proactively. At your visit, request all recommended screenings and update your medication list.

2. Free Cancer Screenings

Colonoscopy (every 10 years), mammogram (annually), lung cancer CT for former smokers 50–77 — all $0 when done preventively. Most seniors skip these because they assume there's a cost.

3. Diabetes Prevention Program

If pre-diabetic, Medicare covers this structured lifestyle program proven to reduce diabetes risk by 58%. Ask your doctor if you qualify.

4. Cardiac Rehabilitation — 36 Sessions

After heart attack, bypass, or stenting — Medicare covers 36 supervised rehab sessions. Fewer than 30% of eligible patients use this benefit that reduces cardiac mortality by 20–30%.

5. Free Mental Health Screening

Annual depression screening is free at the Annual Wellness Visit. If detected, Medicare covers ongoing therapy including telehealth from home.

6. Smoking Cessation — 8 Sessions Free

Up to 8 counseling sessions per year at $0. Ask your doctor for a referral.

7. Diabetic Shoes — 1 Pair Per Year

Beneficiaries with diabetes and peripheral neuropathy get therapeutic shoes and three pairs of insoles per year under Part B. Rarely offered proactively — you must ask.

8. All Vaccines $0 Under Part D

Flu, COVID, Shingrix (shingles — two doses), pneumococcal, RSV, hepatitis B. Shingrix alone has a retail cost of $150+ per dose — $0 under Part D.

9. Chronic Care Management

Two or more chronic conditions? Medicare covers monthly care coordination — medication management, specialist coordination, 24/7 phone access to your care team. Ask your primary care doctor if you qualify.

10. Second Surgical Opinion — $0

Before any elective Medicare-covered surgery, you are entitled to a second opinion at $0. A third opinion is covered at 50% if the first two conflict.

  • Schedule your Annual Wellness Visit — call and use those exact words
  • Ask your Medicare Advantage plan about fitness and transportation benefits
  • Confirm Shingrix is covered — it's $0 under Part D
  • Before any surgery, request your $0 second opinion
